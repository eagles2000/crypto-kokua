Taxes

Crypto taxes, in plain English.

The IRS treats crypto like property. You owe tax when you sell, swap or spend at a gain, not while you hold.

Pledge your coins as collateral and borrow against them. Borrowing isn't a taxable event and you keep any upside. The risk: if prices fall too far, your coins are sold automatically.
