Transparency

How Crypto Kōkua works.

Who runs this site, how it's paid for, where the numbers come from, and what we do and don't do with your information. If you're trusting a site to explain money, you deserve to know.

Free, no ads

All lessons and tools are free. No sign-up, no ads and no paid placements.

No affiliate links

We earn nothing when you click an exchange, wallet or lender. Links go straight to the official site.

No sponsors

No company covered here paid to be included or reviewed what we wrote.

No tracking accounts

No logins, no email list, no selling data. Your settings stay in your own browser.

Who runs it

Crypto Kōkua is built and run by David Cantrell through Shaka Software LLC, a small veteran-owned software company in Kapolei, Oʻahu. David has invested in crypto for years and built this site so beginners can learn without being sold to. Kōkua means help in Hawaiian.

How it's paid for

Shaka Software LLC pays the hosting costs. The lessons, live prices, calculators and free guide carry no ads and no affiliate links. David also offers optional paid services: video setup sessions, Kūpuna crypto help and printed or custom guides. Paying for a service never changes what the lessons say about any company. If partner links are ever added, this page will say so and every such link will be labeled.

How lessons are written and checked

  • Lessons are researched from each company's official site and help center, regulators, and reputable news sources, and written in plain English.
  • Fees, rates and rules change often. Each lesson notes when figures were current, and we aim to update lessons when a company announces a change.
  • The free starter guide carries a version number on its cover so you can tell when it was updated.
  • AI tools help with research and drafting, and figures are checked against the original sources before a lesson goes live.

What we store

  • Your light or dark mode, voice choice and reading speed are saved only in your own browser so they stick between visits. Clearing your browser data removes them.
  • The voice feature uses your device's built-in speech tools. Microphone questions are turned into text by your browser (Chrome and Safari may use Google's or Apple's speech service) and are not sent to us.
  • Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, like any website.
  • The Ask Claude, ChatGPT and Perplexity buttons open those companies' sites with your question filled in. What happens next is covered by their privacy policies. Never type a recovery phrase, password or account number into any AI.

What this site is not

Crypto Kōkua is education, not financial, tax, legal or investment advice. We don't hold your money, don't execute trades, and can't recover a lost wallet. Crypto prices can fall sharply, and you can lose money. Talk to a licensed professional before big decisions.
