Dollar-cost averaging

What if you'd bought a little every week?

Dollar-cost averaging (DCA) means buying a fixed dollar amount on a schedule, no matter the price. Pick a coin and an amount to see how it would have worked over the past year using real prices, compared with investing it all at once on day one.

Why people use DCAIt takes the guessing out of timing. You buy more coins when prices are low and fewer when they're high, and you never put everything in at the top. Many exchanges offer automatic recurring buys, and setting one up with a limit-order or low-fee mode keeps costs down.
