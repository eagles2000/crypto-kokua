Scam check

Got a message that feels off?

"Your account is locked." "Verify your wallet." "I'm from Coinbase support." Paste the message here and get a second opinion in seconds from an AI, or send it to me for a personal answer.

Before you pasteRemove your name, account numbers and phone number. Never paste a recovery phrase, password or 2FA code anywhere, including here. No real company will ever ask for them.

Red flags

  • Urgency: "act now or lose your account"
  • Someone contacted you first, by text, call, email or social media
  • Asks for your recovery phrase, password, codes, or to install a "support" app
  • Promises guaranteed returns or "double your crypto"
  • Asks you to send crypto or buy gift cards to "fix" something
  • Links that look close to a real site but aren't quite right
