1Robinhood at a Glance

Robinhood (Brokerage, Crypto and Robinhood Wallet)

robinhood.com | Robinhood app and the separate Robinhood Wallet app

Go to Robinhood Crypto ↗ About Robinhood Wallet ↗

Robinhood is the popular U.S. investing app based in Menlo Park, California. One app holds stocks, ETFs, options and crypto. It also makes a second, separate app called Robinhood Wallet. The two work very differently, and mixing them up is the most common beginner mistake.

Side 1: the Robinhood app (custodial)

Availability
Robinhood Crypto is available in every U.S. state, including Hawaiʻi, plus D.C., Puerto Rico and the U.S. Virgin Islands. Not every coin is offered in every state.
Coins
About 90 tradable coins, including Bitcoin, Ethereum, XRP, Solana, Dogecoin, Cardano, Litecoin, Avalanche, Stellar, Chainlink and Shiba Inu.
Fees
Two ways to trade. The default market-maker route shows no commission, but Robinhood is paid inside the price spread: as of June 2026 it receives $0.95 for every $100 traded. Turning on exchange routing (Account > Settings > Crypto > Fee tiers) switches to a visible fee of 0% to 0.95%, lower as your 30-day volume grows, with seven tiers down to 0.03% for big traders. Compare the coins you would receive before you confirm.
Staking
Earn rewards on Ethereum, Solana and Cardano starting with $1. ETH has a waiting period before rewards start, and unstaking takes time before coins are available.
Transfers
Send coins out to your own wallet or receive them in. Robinhood charges no deposit or withdrawal fee; you pay the network fee. Only send to your Receive address; the address Robinhood sends from is different.
Also offers
Stocks, ETFs (including all 13 U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs), options, the Robinhood Gold membership, and Robinhood Legend, a desktop trading platform with advanced charts.

Side 2: Robinhood Wallet (self-custody)

A free, standalone app (iPhone and Android) where the keys live on your phone and you keep the 12-word recovery phrase. Robinhood cannot see it or reset it. It supports the Ethereum, Bitcoin, Solana, Dogecoin, Arbitrum, Polygon, Optimism and Base networks, and can receive on Robinhood Chain. You can swap tokens inside the app and connect to decentralized apps. It does not hold XRP, so keep XRP in the Robinhood app, Exodus or Edge.

Robinhood Chain (the Arbitrum connection)

Robinhood Chain is Robinhood's own blockchain. It is an Ethereum "layer 2," built with Arbitrum's technology, so it settles to Ethereum but runs faster and cheaper. It opened for public testing in February 2026 and launched on mainnet on July 1, 2026. It is designed for tokenized assets such as Stock Tokens (shares of U.S. stocks represented as tokens that can trade around the clock), plus decentralized apps like Uniswap. Robinhood's earlier Stock Tokens in Europe ran on Arbitrum One starting in June 2025. You reach Robinhood Chain through the Robinhood Wallet. Availability of Stock Tokens and some on-chain products depends on where you live.

How to get started

  1. Robinhood app: download Robinhood, sign up with your email, verify your ID and SSN, and link your bank. Turn on 2FA with an authenticator app.
  2. Tap a coin, then Buy. For lower fees, check the fee-tier setting first.
  3. Robinhood Wallet: download the separate Robinhood Wallet app (publisher: Robinhood), create a wallet, and write the 12 words on paper.
  4. To move coins to the Wallet, tap Receive in the Wallet to copy its address, then send from the Robinhood app on the same network. Test with a small amount first.
Know which app you are inCoins in the Robinhood app are held by Robinhood; coins in Robinhood Wallet are held by you. Robinhood support can help with the first but can never recover a Wallet if you lose the 12 words, and Robinhood will never ask you for them. Fake "Robinhood Wallet" apps and support numbers exist, so download only from the official app store listing.

2Words to Know

TermMeaning
CustodialA company holds your crypto for you, like the Robinhood app or an exchange.
Self-custodyYou hold your own keys and recovery phrase, like Robinhood Wallet or Exodus.
Recovery phraseThe 12 words that restore a self-custody wallet. Anyone with them controls the money.
SpreadThe gap between the buy and sell price. Some apps earn their fee inside it.
StakingLocking certain coins to help secure a network in exchange for rewards.
Layer 2A faster, cheaper network that runs on top of Ethereum and settles back to it. Arbitrum and Robinhood Chain are examples.
Tokenized stockA token on a blockchain that represents a share of a real stock.
