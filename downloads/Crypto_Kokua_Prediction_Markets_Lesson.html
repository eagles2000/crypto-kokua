1How Prediction Markets Work

Kalshi ↗ Polymarket ↗

A prediction market lets you buy a contract on whether something will happen: will the Fed cut rates, will Bitcoin close above a price, who wins a game or an election. Each contract pays $1 if you're right and $0 if you're wrong.

How the price works

The price is the crowd's guess at the odds. A "Yes" contract at 30¢ means traders think there's about a 30% chance. Buy 100 Yes contracts at 30¢ for $30. If it happens you collect $100; if not, you lose the $30. You can also sell before the event if the price moves your way.

Other places you'll see them

Several apps you may already use offer event contracts, often built on Kalshi: Coinbase, Robinhood, Crypto.com and Gemini, plus sportsbook companies such as DraftKings and Fanatics.

State rules are in flux

Prediction markets are federally regulated, but many states argue that sports contracts are really gambling, and dozens of lawsuits are underway. Some states block certain markets or the whole platform, and the lists change. Each app checks your location at sign-up, so trust what it says for your state.

Taxes

Winnings are taxable even if you never get a tax form. The IRS hasn't issued one clear rule, so tax pros disagree on whether they count as capital gains, special futures treatment, or gambling. Keep your statements and ask a tax professional if you trade a lot.

Treat it like entertainment moneyMany contracts end at $0. Only use money you can afford to lose, set a monthly limit, and remember the person on the other side of your trade may know more than you. If it stops being fun, both Kalshi and Polymarket let you set limits or take a break.

For help with problem gambling, call or text 1-800-GAMBLER.

Kalshi: sign up and place your first trade

Kalshi ↗ Kalshi help ↗

Kalshi is a U.S. exchange for event contracts, regulated by the CFTC since 2020. You trade against other people, not against Kalshi.

Have these readyEmail, mobile phone, a government photo ID, your home address, and a bank account or debit card. You must be 18 or older.

Open your account

  1. Go to kalshi.com (type it yourself) or download the Kalshi app and tap Sign up.
  2. Enter your email and a strong password you don't use anywhere else, then verify your phone.
  3. Complete identity verification with your name, address, date of birth and photo ID. Kalshi may ask for your SSN for tax reporting.
  4. Check that your state is allowed. Kalshi blocks some markets or the whole app in a few states, and the list changes.
  5. Turn on two-factor authentication in your account security settings if offered, using an authenticator app.

Fund it

  • Bank transfer (ACH): $10 minimum, no fee. Takes 1 to 3 business days to withdraw.
  • Also: debit card (fee), Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, Venmo, wire, or USDC crypto.

Place a trade

  1. Open a market, such as "Will the Fed cut rates in March?"
  2. Choose Yes or No. The price (for example 42¢) is the cost of one contract that pays $1.
  3. For lower fees, use a limit order: set your own price and wait. Kalshi charges takers (instant orders) a fee that's largest near 50¢ prices, and makers (resting limit orders) little or nothing on many markets.
  4. Review the maximum loss and maximum payout, then confirm.
  5. Sell any time before the market closes, or hold until it settles.
Watch the fees on toss-upsTrading fees are highest when prices are near 50/50. Start small, use limit orders, and read each market's exact rules: they define precisely what counts as "Yes."

Polymarket: the U.S. app and how to start

Polymarket ↗ Find it in the App Store ↗

Polymarket started in 2020 as a crypto-based site and became the world's largest prediction market. It left the U.S. in 2022, then bought the CFTC-licensed exchange QCEX and came back with a separate Polymarket US app. The waitlist ended in May 2026.

Only use the U.S. app. Using a VPN or crypto wallet to reach the international site from the U.S. breaks its terms and could get funds frozen.

Sign up

  1. On an iPhone, open the App Store and install Polymarket (check the publisher). An Android app was still coming as of mid-2026.
  2. Sign in with your Apple ID or Google account. Make sure that account has 2FA turned on, since it now protects your Polymarket money.
  3. Pick a username and enter your full name, date of birth and home address. Most identity checks pass instantly.
  4. The app checks your state. Several states, such as Nevada, aren't available.
  5. Add money with the options the app shows, then browse markets.

Trading

  • Prices work the same as Kalshi: a Yes contract at 30¢ pays $1 if it happens.
  • Fees are charged per trade and are highest when the price is near 50/50.
  • Most U.S. markets are sports, with elections, economics, crypto and culture too.
Same rules as any marketStart small, set a monthly limit, and read each market's resolution rules before you buy. Winnings are taxable.
